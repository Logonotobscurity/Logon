# Cross-Domain Research Crosswalk — 2026-09-29

## NIST AI RMF
Use NIST AI RMF as the governance framing layer for identifying, measuring, managing and governing AI risk. The framework is voluntary and NIST is revising AI RMF 1.0; a Critical Infrastructure Profile concept note was released in April 2026.
Source: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

## OWASP Agentic Security
Persistent memory/context and tool output are security-relevant inputs. Research notebooks therefore require provenance, boundary validation and auditability before retrieved context is treated as trusted knowledge.
Source: https://genai.owasp.org/

## MCP 2026-07-28
New LOG_ON MCP integrations should avoid deprecated Roots, Sampling, Logging and legacy HTTP+SSE. Current MCP guidance also moves Tasks into an extension and strengthens OAuth/client metadata requirements.
Source: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/

## OpenRouter
Provider routing should consider data policy before quality/cost/latency. OpenRouter documents provider-level data-collection filtering; the deny mode excludes providers that may store user data non-transiently.
Sources:
https://openrouter.ai/privacy/
https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/docs/blob/main/guides/routing/provider-selection.mdx

## African NLP
INJONGO demonstrates the importance of native-speaker African-language utterances for intent and slot evaluation rather than relying only on translated English benchmarks.
Source:
https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.464/

## Structured uncertainty
Suri et al. provide a parameter-level uncertainty and EVPI-based framework for choosing clarification questions in ambiguous tool-calling agents. This provides a concrete research basis for replacing a single scalar confidence threshold with structured uncertainty.
Source:
https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2028/

## Human-AI collaborative evaluation
The supplied ACM TiiS study supports evaluating AI-assisted creative systems through behavioral/cognitive outcomes in addition to output metrics.
Source:
https://doi.org/10.1145/3773292
